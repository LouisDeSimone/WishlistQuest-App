# Wishlist Quest — How to Install (Windows)

A desktop app to track your Steam wishlist growth like an RPG campaign.
**Windows 10/11.** You don't need to install anything else.

---

## 1. Download

Go to the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)** and, under **Assets**,
download the file ending in **`-setup.exe`**.

## 2. Run the installer

Double-click the file you downloaded.

> **You'll probably see a blue "Windows protected your PC" box.** That's normal
> for a new indie app that isn't code-signed yet — Windows just doesn't recognize
> the publisher. It is **not** a virus alert about this app.
>
> Click **More info** → then **Run anyway**.

Then click through the wizard and **Install**. It installs **just for you** — no
admin password, no IT permissions.

## 3. Open it

Launch **Wishlist Quest** from your **Start Menu** (type "Wishlist Quest").

A thin **March Bar** appears at the bottom of your screen — that's your campaign
companion. Click **Open App** on it for the full dashboard.

## 4. Start your campaign

- Add your game and your current wishlist total — you'll start leveling up,
  unlocking forts, and marching toward your goal.
- Log your wishlist totals over time to earn XP and keep your streak.

## 5. (Optional) Connect Steam for automatic numbers

If you have a Steam partner account, you can auto-pull your daily wishlist
numbers:

1. Open the dashboard → **Steam Sync**.
2. Enter your **Steam App ID** and your **Steam Financial API key**.

> **Your key never leaves your computer.** It's stored in Windows Credential
> Manager on your own PC and is only used by the app locally — it is never sent
> to anyone, never shown again, and never included in backups. After that, the
> app syncs once a day on its own.

Don't have a partner key? No problem — manual logging works great without it.

---

### Good to know

- **Your data stays on your device** (local only). Use **Export backup** in the
  footer to move it to another PC or keep a copy.
- **Updates are automatic.** When a new version ships, you'll see a
  **"Reinforcements have arrived"** banner the next time you open the app — click
  **Install & Restart** and you're done. Your data is kept. (No need to come back
  here and re-download.)
- **Mac/Linux:** not available yet (Windows only for now).
